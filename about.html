TWO PEOPLE WHO GOT TIRED OF DIGITAL ADS NOBODY WATCHES.

We started BACKPANEL because every startup founder we met had the same complaint: "My ads get skipped, scrolled past, or blocked." So we put the ad where nobody can skip it — the back of an auto-rickshaw at a red light.

Why auto-rickshaws?

Three reasons. None of them are romantic.

  • Nobody can skip it. The driver behind you is stuck at a red light. No ad-blocker. No "Skip Ad" button. Just your brand, staring them in the face for 90 seconds.
  • It's hyper-local. We blanket the one neighbourhood you sell in. Not "Gujarat-wide awareness." Just the 25 autos that pass your customer's office every day.
  • It's affordable. ≈₹5 CPM, derived from our own rates. Your performance marketer just winced. With jealousy.

What we don't do

Boundaries are a feature, not a limitation.

  • No hood stickers. Back-panel only. RTO sign-off, per auto, per city. No gray zone.
  • No LED. No moving QR. Static vinyl and a link. That's it.
  • No fake numbers. Every stat on this site is derived from our own rates or labeled as an estimate. We publish the math. You can check it.
  • No spam. We don't sell your data, we don't send you 47 emails, and we don't add you to a WhatsApp group you never asked for.
