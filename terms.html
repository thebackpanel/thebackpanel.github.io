Terms of Service: boring, but fair.

We put ads on autos. You pay for autos with ads. Everything below just says that in longer words.

Service description

BACKPANEL runs RTO-certified back-panel advertising on auto-rickshaws in Bengaluru (currently live across Gujarat, expanding to Bengaluru), plus optional extras from our Stack page: NFC tap cards, neon signs, reel shoots, influencer visits, social setup, and WhatsApp kits. Campaigns include print on eco-vinyl, installation, RTO/municipal sign-off per auto, ad tax, GPS proof, and a founder pitch video where stated.

Quote & campaigns

Every quote is custom to hood, auto count, and duration. But the floor is fixed, as stated on pricing: minimum 10 autos, 3-month run. Quotes are valid 14 days. Campaigns start only after creative approval and payment confirmation. Timelines for print/install are estimates; traffic, RTO queues, and rain get a vote too. We share GPS and photo proof on launch.

Pitch on Wheels contest basics

Pitch on Wheels is our rolling founder-video contest. You pitch, we shoot/edit, the street watches. Founders keep the footage: repost it, run it as an ad, pin it, it is yours. Best entries, as picked by us plus audience response, win free back-panel slots. Entry does not guarantee a win or a campaign slot. Keep it legal, keep it yours (no plagiarised scripts or music you do not own).

Compliance

RTO back-panel only. No hood stickers, no LED screens, no moving/animation, no oversized panels beyond the permitted ad area (75% rule). Static vinyl + QR link only. We carry the NOC, pay the ad tax, and brief creatives so they pass ASCI and municipal checks. No fake stats, no greenwashing, and AI-assisted artwork is labelled per ASCI's 2026 draft. If an authority flags a creative, we revise once free and fast.
