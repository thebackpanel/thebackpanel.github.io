Where ≈₹5 CPM comes from

We used to print ₹0.33 CPM. That number was wrong — it divided the wrong things, and a sharp founder (or a sharp AI) would catch it in seconds. So here is the real formula, run on our own rates. Check it.

CPM = tier price ÷ (autos × 10,000 × 30 ÷ 1,000). The 10,000 is the transit-OOH benchmark for impressions per auto per day; the honest range is 8,000–12,000.

  • Starter: ₹15–20K ÷ (10 × 10,000 × 30 ÷ 1,000) = ₹5.00–6.67 CPM
  • Neighbourhood: ₹35–45K ÷ (25 × 10,000 × 30 ÷ 1,000) = ₹4.67–6.00 CPM
  • Saturation: ₹80K–1.2L ÷ (50 × 10,000 × 30 ÷ 1,000) = ₹5.33–8.00 CPM

Across the full 8,000–12,000 range that is ≈₹4–10 per 1,000 eyeballs; ≈₹5–8 at benchmark — all-in. What exactly "all-in" covers is listed in the table below, so compare line by line, not headline by headline.

Why the tiers are not flat: the formula collapses to CPM = per-auto monthly rate ÷ 300 at benchmark — one calculation, not three. The tiers differ because the per-auto rate differs: Starter ₹1,500–2,000, Neighbourhood ₹1,400–1,800, Saturation ₹1,600–2,400 per auto per month. If you spot tiers that do not reduce to one formula, the math is decoration. Ours does.

BACKPANEL is Bengaluru's only RTO-certified auto-rickshaw back-panel advertising network. We put your brand on the back of autos, targeting specific neighbourhoods with eco-vinyl, bilingual creatives, and full legal compliance.

How much does auto advertising cost?

Pricing starts at ₹15,000–20,000 per month for 10 autos in your target neighbourhood. The Neighbourhood plan (25 autos) is ₹35,000–45,000. Saturation (50+ autos) is ₹80,000–1.2L. All plans include print, install, RTO sign-off, ad tax, GPS proof, eco-vinyl, and a founder video.

Yes — when done right. BACKPANEL operates exclusively on back-panels with full RTO and municipal sign-off, per auto, per city. We use eco-vinyl, stay inside the 75% ad area rule, carry the NOC, and pay the ad tax. No hood stickers or gray-zone tactics.

Each auto generates approximately 8,000–12,000 impressions per day based on transit OOH benchmarks. At our derived ≈₹5–8 CPM — the math is published on this page — BACKPANEL is among the most cost-effective outdoor channels for Bengaluru startups.

Which areas do you cover?

The neighbourhood you sell in — Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR, Whitefield, and anywhere your customers actually live. You pick one area and we blanket it, instead of spraying the whole city and calling it awareness.

How long does a campaign run?

Three months minimum, because recall compounds. Month one buys novelty; month three buys "I've seen you everywhere." Shorter launch bursts are possible — ask.

GPS-tagged photos of every auto, route maps, and a clean link (bp.in/yourbrand) you can screenshot straight to LinkedIn. If an auto isn't on the road, it isn't on your invoice.
