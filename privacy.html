Privacy Policy: we collect almost nothing, on purpose.

Short version: no cookies, no ad trackers, no data sale. If you email us, we reply. That is the whole business model for your personal data — there isn't one.

What we collect

Only what you deliberately send us: your name, brand name, email, target hood/city, and whatever one-liner you put in the contact form. If you email founders@backpanel.in, we get that email. Nothing else. No background harvesting, no enrichment, no detective work.

What we never collect

  • No cookies. We do not set first-party or third-party cookies for tracking, ads, or profiling.
  • No ads trackers. No Meta pixel, no Google ads tags, no cross-site fingerprinting on this site.
  • No sale, no rental. We never sell, rent, or trade your contact details. Your inbox is yours.

Forms & contact

Our forms submit via Google Forms and/or open your mail app with a pre-filled mailto: to founders@backpanel.in. That means: if you use Google Forms, Google's own privacy policy applies to that submission; if you use mailto, your email provider handles it. We keep replies in our inbox and a private tracking sheet so we actually reply within a day. We do not plug your email into a drip campaign unless you explicitly ask.

Analytics

We use a privacy-first, provider-agnostic event shim: plain counts like "someone clicked Get Wrapped" with no personal data, no IP storage, no user IDs, no session replay. If we ever switch analytics providers, the rule stays the same — aggregate events only, nothing that identifies you. Do Not Track? We were already not tracking.

Your rights + DPDP Act 2023 note for India

You can ask to see, correct, or delete whatever you sent us, any time — just email founders@backpanel.in with "Delete my data" and we will do it and confirm. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have rights to access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, and to withdraw consent. We process your enquiry details only to respond to you (consent + legitimate use), keep them only as long as needed for the enquiry, and never use them for an unrelated purpose. Grievance? Same inbox: founders answer it themselves.

Contact

Privacy questions, deletion requests, complaints, compliments: founders@backpanel.in. A human founder replies, usually within 24 hours.
